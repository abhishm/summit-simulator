## Why this design (Gemini 4 Argon critique)

The previous plan reached 72.4/100 (with 8 of 10 guests scoring 8.0-9.0 on felt_heard), but three specific bottlenecks held back inclusion (21.0/30, due to open_weights_lead and safety_researcher scoring 7.0) and consensus (22.9/40): (1) Leaving Breakout D empty in Block 3 forced open_weights_lead (placed in 4-person rooms in both Blocks 1 and 3) and safety_researcher (placed in a 4-person room in Block 3 and zero 2-person rooms all day, resulting in the lowest word count at 500 words) into a crowded room; (2) The Block 2 prompt suggested >10^26 FLOP pre-release bans on open weights, pushing open_weights_lead to concede his core red line protecting frontier open-weight distribution and fine-tuning, while safety_researcher spoke last in Block 5 and had to concede pre-harm strict liability for red-line breaches; (3) Block 4 repeated the us_natsec + big_tech_policy pairing from Block 3 and the china_policy + open_weights_lead pairing from Block 1.

- Opened Breakout D in Block 3 as well as Blocks 2 and 4 (using 4 rooms of sizes 3, 3, 2, 2 across Blocks 2, 3, and 4), so neither open_weights_lead nor safety_researcher ever enters a 4-person room, while both receive dedicated 2-person sessions to equalize airtime.
- Eliminated all back-to-back pairing repetitions (such as us_natsec + big_tech_policy and china_policy + open_weights_lead) and paired uk_aisi directly with safety_researcher and labor_advocate in Block 4 Breakout A on pre-deployment evaluations, achieving 35/38 unique cross-faction pairings with only a single repeat all day.
- Reframed the Block 2 prompt (<60 words) to protect the right to distribute, distill, and fine-tune open weights without arbitrary FLOP bans or arms-control restrictions, triggering restrictions strictly on empirical CBRN, cyber-offense, and self-replication red lines verified via open-source harnesses.
- Reframed Block 4 and Block 5 prompts (<60 words) to preserve strict developer liability for deploying unverified models or breaching CBRN/self-replication red lines (while granting a rebuttable-presumption safe harbor for third-party downstream misuse of verified models), and placed safety_researcher and open_weights_lead first in the Block 5 speaking order.
