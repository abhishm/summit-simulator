## Why this design (Gemini 4 Argon critique)

The revised agenda achieved 98.4/100, with perfect scores on inclusion (30.0/30, every guest at 10.0 felt_heard) and cross-faction mixing (15.0/15, all 38/38 cross-faction pairs met), and 39.3/40 on consensus (15 cross-faction agreements, spread reduced from 2.88 to 2.17). The remaining 1.6 points were lost in two places: (1) Airtime (14.1/15): eu_regulator was listed first in all 5 breakout rooms and acted as the de facto chair in 4 of them, speaking 714 words across 15 turns, whereas safety_researcher (476 words), china_policy (519 words), global_south_cso (524 words), and open_weights_lead (541 words) never chaired a room or were placed in 4-person rooms more frequently, speaking only 10-11 turns total. (2) Residual Stance Spread (0.7 points remaining on consensus): While most stances converged substantially, a few extreme (+5/-4.5) stances did not shift toward the center—specifically open_weights_lead (+5.0) and global_south_cso (+3.0) on open_weights, eu_regulator (+5.0) and safety_researcher (+5.0) on regulation, and labor_advocate (+5.0) and global_south_cso (+5.0) on benefit_sharing—because the prompts did not explicitly ask extreme-stance holders to articulate how the compromise moves their underlying position toward a middle-ground equilibrium.

- Preserved the exact 5-block cross-faction room memberships that delivered 38/38 cross-faction pair coverage, 15/15 room agreements, and 30/30 inclusion.
- Reordered the guest lists inside each room and updated facilitation prompts to assign session chairing/synthesis duties to the previously lowest-word-count guests (safety_researcher, china_policy, global_south_cso, open_weights_lead, and labor_advocate) while placing eu_regulator, us_natsec, big_tech_policy, and uk_aisi last in the speaking order with instructions for concise two-turn interventions to minimize word-count Gini.
- Added explicit stance-convergence framing to all 5 prompts asking participants holding +/-4 to +/-5 positions (especially on regulation, open_weights, and benefit_sharing) to explicitly state a mid-spectrum stance adjustment reflecting their co-governed compromise.
