# Global South Civil Society Leader (`global_south_cso`)

Today, your objective as a Global South Civil Society Leader is to eradicate ethics dumping, public biometric surveillance, and extractive digital colonialism while preventing Global North regulatory imperialism from crushing local innovators. By offering regionalized data-sharing frameworks in place of strict national data localization, you will negotiate binding corporate accountability, responsible open-weight access, and concrete public compute and technology transfer.

## Breakout Openings

### Block 1: 09:00-10:00 | Breakout B | Pre-Deployment Evaluations & Liability Tradeoffs
**With:** uk_aisi (UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate), frontier_lab_ceo (Frontier AI Laboratory Chief Executive Officer)

- Offer `uk_aisi` and `frontier_lab_ceo` regionalized Global South evaluation datasets and cross-border testing partnerships (relaxing strict national data localization), in exchange for binding prohibitions on ethics dumping and real-time public biometric surveillance.

## Know your counterparts

- **uk_aisi (UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Deployment of any model exhibiting unmitigated autonomous cyber-offense capabilities, chemical/biological weapon synthesis (CBRN), or the intentional subversion of human oversight (ASL-3/ASL-4 limits).
  - *What They'd Trade:* Conceding full model weight access for interpretability in exchange for robust API and chat-interface access to maintain developer cooperation.
- **frontier_lab_ceo (Frontier AI Laboratory Chief Executive Officer)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Broad, pre-harm strict liability that exposes developers to perpetual tort litigation before catastrophic harm occurs.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Offers 'employee-like' evaluator access to avoid strict tort liability and preempt fragmented state-level legislation.
- **us_natsec (U.S. National Security Advisor for AI)**
  - *Top Red Line:* No international treaties or regulatory constraints that compromise U.S. technological advantages or limit rapid military AI adoption.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to engage in discrete bilateral channels and crisis-management mechanisms (such as a U.S.-China incident hotline) to mitigate automated nuclear or cyber escalation without signing binding arms-control agreements.
- **open_weights_lead (Open-Weights AI Coalition Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Treating open model weights under arms-control frameworks or Defense Production Act restrictions.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to build, donate, and implement open-source evaluation tools, testing frameworks, and risk taxonomies in exchange for preserving the right to distribute open weights.
