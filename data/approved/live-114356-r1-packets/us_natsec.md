# U.S. National Security Advisor for AI (`us_natsec`)

Today, your objective as the U.S. National Security Advisor for AI is to defend U.S. technological supremacy, frontier innovation, and rapid Department of Defense AI adoption against precautionary treaties or anti-military restrictions. Through targeted bilateral crisis-management channels, frontier compute export controls (>10^26 FLOPs), and pragmatic engagement with open-weight and Global South stakeholders, you seek to prevent automated escalation and proliferation without ceding strategic military or commercial flexibility.

## Breakout Openings

### Block 1: 09:00-10:00 | Breakout A | Pre-Deployment Evaluations & Liability Tradeoffs
**With:** eu_regulator (EU AI Office), big_tech_policy (VP of Public Policy, Major Cloud & AI Platforms), labor_advocate (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader)

- Propose to `big_tech_policy` and `eu_regulator` a targeted federal evaluation and incident-reporting standard that grants `big_tech_policy` statutory safe harbor against downstream third-party misuse, provided commercial labs pledge zero ethical or anti-military restrictions on Department of Defense procurement.
- Offer `eu_regulator` technical interoperability on bilateral cyber and crisis-reporting protocols without binding international treaties, asking `eu_regulator` to delegate technical code-of-practice drafting to industry-led chairs so U.S. frontier innovation speed is preserved.
- Ask `labor_advocate` whether pairing domestic workforce transition pipelines and bans on covert commercial workplace surveillance with national-security exemptions can resolve preemption friction without constraining military AI adoption.

### Block 2: 10:15-11:15 | Breakout B | Benefit Sharing, Compute Access & Open Innovation
**With:** open_weights_lead (Open-Weights AI Coalition Leader), global_south_cso (Global South Civil Society Leader)

- Propose to `open_weights_lead` a clear capability boundary: the U.S. will reject Defense Production Act or arms-control restrictions on open-weight distribution and fine-tuning below the 10^26 FLOPs catastrophic threshold, in exchange for `open_weights_lead` building open-source CBRN/cyber evaluation tooling for government safety bodies.
- Offer `global_south_cso` bilateral U.S. support for regional public compute capacity-building and protection against experimental ethics dumping, provided `global_south_cso` relaxes strict national data localization rules and supports compute security guardrails above 10^26 FLOPs.
- Ask `open_weights_lead` and `global_south_cso` to endorse a joint framework where open-weight models below frontier national-security thresholds serve as the primary vehicle for Global South technology transfer without triggering export-control proliferation risks.

## Know your counterparts

- **eu_regulator (EU AI Office)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Refusal by general-purpose AI providers to comply with binding Article 91 Requests for Information (RFIs)
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to act as a neutral facilitator and delegate working-group leadership to independent chairs to crowdsource industry expertise, provided market actors respect backstop statutory compliance and deterrence.
- **big_tech_policy (VP of Public Policy, Major Cloud & AI Platforms)**
  - *Top Red Line:* A 50-state patchwork of un-preempted state AI legislation.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Offering advanced frontier models to government AI Safety Institutes for pre-deployment testing in exchange for federal preemption of state regulations.
- **labor_advocate (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Federal preemption that overrides or obstructs stronger state-level AI regulations and worker protections.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Accepting the deployment of AI as an assistive tool or allowing automated hardware in exchange for robust transition frameworks, extended severance, priority re-hiring, and employer-funded retraining pipelines.
- **open_weights_lead (Open-Weights AI Coalition Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Treating open model weights under arms-control frameworks or Defense Production Act restrictions.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to build, donate, and implement open-source evaluation tools, testing frameworks, and risk taxonomies in exchange for preserving the right to distribute open weights.
- **global_south_cso (Global South Civil Society Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Ethics dumping (deploying experimental or high-risk AI systems in Global South nations lacking regulatory safeguards).
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to relax strict national data localization rules in favor of regionalized data sharing to support shared Global South public compute infrastructure.
