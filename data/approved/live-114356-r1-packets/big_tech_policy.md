# VP of Public Policy, Major Cloud & AI Platforms (`big_tech_policy`)

Today, your objective as VP of Public Policy for Major Cloud & AI Platforms is to establish a FINRA-style two-track regulatory framework that pairs pre-deployment frontier model testing, incident reporting, and watermarking with federal preemption of state patchworks and statutory safe harbors against downstream misuse liability. Across both breakout sessions, you must navigate labor demands on workplace surveillance and creative consent without surrendering fair-use training protections.

## Breakout Openings

### Block 1: 09:00-10:00 | Breakout A | Pre-Deployment Evaluations & Liability Tradeoffs
**With:** eu_regulator (EU AI Office), us_natsec (U.S. National Security Advisor for AI), labor_advocate (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader)

- Offer `eu_regulator` and `us_natsec` mandatory pre-deployment frontier model testing via government AISIs, standardized incident reporting, and content watermarking, in exchange for federal preemption of fragmented 50-state patchworks and statutory safe harbors against third-party misuse liability.
- Propose to `labor_advocate` a binding commitment prohibiting covert workplace biometric/keystroke surveillance and guaranteeing human-in-the-loop appeal rights for employment algorithms, provided workforce rules are harmonized into a uniform national standard rather than a 50-state compliance patchwork.
- Ask `us_natsec` and `eu_regulator` to protect fair-use training data practices and avoid input-based architectural micromanagement, focusing oversight strictly on output evaluations and FINRA-style co-regulatory standards.

### Block 2: 10:15-11:15 | Breakout C | Benefit Sharing, Compute Access & Open Innovation
**With:** uk_aisi (UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate), labor_advocate (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader)

- Offer `uk_aisi` seamless pre-deployment API testing access and automated watermarking/incident telemetry, in exchange for `uk_aisi` certifying compliance under a statutory safe harbor that shields developers from strict liability for downstream third-party jailbreaks.
- Propose to `labor_advocate` an employer-funded workforce retraining pipeline, union audit rights over internal operational models, and cryptographic provenance tools to support creative residuals—in exchange for preserving fair-use model training and predictable national regulatory standards.
- Ask `uk_aisi` and `labor_advocate` how a two-track regulatory model can pair high-bar frontier safety and worker transition guarantees with streamlined federal compliance that avoids state-by-state litigation.

## Know your counterparts

- **eu_regulator (EU AI Office)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Refusal by general-purpose AI providers to comply with binding Article 91 Requests for Information (RFIs)
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to act as a neutral facilitator and delegate working-group leadership to independent chairs to crowdsource industry expertise, provided market actors respect backstop statutory compliance and deterrence.
- **us_natsec (U.S. National Security Advisor for AI)**
  - *Top Red Line:* No international treaties or regulatory constraints that compromise U.S. technological advantages or limit rapid military AI adoption.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to engage in discrete bilateral channels and crisis-management mechanisms (such as a U.S.-China incident hotline) to mitigate automated nuclear or cyber escalation without signing binding arms-control agreements.
- **labor_advocate (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Federal preemption that overrides or obstructs stronger state-level AI regulations and worker protections.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Accepting the deployment of AI as an assistive tool or allowing automated hardware in exchange for robust transition frameworks, extended severance, priority re-hiring, and employer-funded retraining pipelines.
- **uk_aisi (UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Deployment of any model exhibiting unmitigated autonomous cyber-offense capabilities, chemical/biological weapon synthesis (CBRN), or the intentional subversion of human oversight (ASL-3/ASL-4 limits).
  - *What They'd Trade:* Conceding full model weight access for interpretability in exchange for robust API and chat-interface access to maintain developer cooperation.
