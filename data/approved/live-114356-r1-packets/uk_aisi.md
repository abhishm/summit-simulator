# UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate (`uk_aisi`)

Today, your objective as the UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate is to secure empirical, pre-deployment evaluation access and agentic testing protocols across frontier developers while preventing ASL-3/ASL-4 catastrophic risks in CBRN, cyber-offense, and loss of control. By trading statutory enforcement friction and full weight access for robust API/embedded research access, you will broker practical liability and benefit-sharing compromises between industry labs, labor, and the Global South.

## Breakout Openings

### Block 1: 09:00-10:00 | Breakout B | Pre-Deployment Evaluations & Liability Tradeoffs
**With:** frontier_lab_ceo (Frontier AI Laboratory Chief Executive Officer), global_south_cso (Global South Civil Society Leader)

- Offer `frontier_lab_ceo` a formal guarantee that `uk_aisi` will not demand full raw model weight access or broad pre-harm strict tort liability, in exchange for embedded, 'employee-like' pre-deployment API and agentic evaluation access plus binding internal kill switches for ASL-3/ASL-4 CBRN and cyber risks.
- Propose to `global_south_cso` and `frontier_lab_ceo` that pre-deployment AISI safety evaluations incorporate mandatory red-teaming against Global South ethics dumping and public biometric surveillance before any commercial model rollout.
- Ask `global_south_cso` if anchoring corporate accountability in empirical pre-deployment AISI testing—rather than heavy upfront compliance bureaucracy for small developers—can protect local Global South innovators from regulatory imperialism.

### Block 2: 10:15-11:15 | Breakout C | Benefit Sharing, Compute Access & Open Innovation
**With:** big_tech_policy (VP of Public Policy, Major Cloud & AI Platforms), labor_advocate (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader)

- Offer `big_tech_policy` coordinated, one-stop pre-deployment AISI evaluation protocols that support statutory safe harbors against downstream third-party jailbreaks, provided `big_tech_policy` guarantees uninterrupted pre-deployment testing access and mandatory incident reporting.
- Propose to `labor_advocate` and `big_tech_policy` a windfall-supported workforce transition and retraining framework paired with union audit rights over workplace operational models, resolving federal preemption deadlocks via sector-specific labor floors.
- Ask `big_tech_policy` to commit to transparent watermarking and provenance tools that enable `labor_advocate`'s creative consent and financial residual mechanisms without micromanaging core model architecture.

## Know your counterparts

- **frontier_lab_ceo (Frontier AI Laboratory Chief Executive Officer)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Broad, pre-harm strict liability that exposes developers to perpetual tort litigation before catastrophic harm occurs.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Offers 'employee-like' evaluator access to avoid strict tort liability and preempt fragmented state-level legislation.
- **global_south_cso (Global South Civil Society Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Ethics dumping (deploying experimental or high-risk AI systems in Global South nations lacking regulatory safeguards).
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to relax strict national data localization rules in favor of regionalized data sharing to support shared Global South public compute infrastructure.
- **big_tech_policy (VP of Public Policy, Major Cloud & AI Platforms)**
  - *Top Red Line:* A 50-state patchwork of un-preempted state AI legislation.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Offering advanced frontier models to government AI Safety Institutes for pre-deployment testing in exchange for federal preemption of state regulations.
- **labor_advocate (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Federal preemption that overrides or obstructs stronger state-level AI regulations and worker protections.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Accepting the deployment of AI as an assistive tool or allowing automated hardware in exchange for robust transition frameworks, extended severance, priority re-hiring, and employer-funded retraining pipelines.
