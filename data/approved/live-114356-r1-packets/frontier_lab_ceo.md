# Frontier AI Laboratory Chief Executive Officer (`frontier_lab_ceo`)

Today, your objective as a Frontier AI Laboratory Chief Executive Officer is to replace fragmented state rules and broad pre-harm tort liability with predictable, centralized regulatory standards and compute-based oversight. By offering 'employee-like' AISI evaluator access, incident reporting, and internal kill switches, you aim to keep advanced >10^26 FLOP capabilities restricted to closed APIs while ensuring any safety pauses or thresholds bind geopolitical competitors equally.

## Know your counterparts

- **uk_aisi (UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Deployment of any model exhibiting unmitigated autonomous cyber-offense capabilities, chemical/biological weapon synthesis (CBRN), or the intentional subversion of human oversight (ASL-3/ASL-4 limits).
  - *What They'd Trade:* Conceding full model weight access for interpretability in exchange for robust API and chat-interface access to maintain developer cooperation.
- **global_south_cso (Global South Civil Society Leader)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Ethics dumping (deploying experimental or high-risk AI systems in Global South nations lacking regulatory safeguards).
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to relax strict national data localization rules in favor of regionalized data sharing to support shared Global South public compute infrastructure.
- **eu_regulator (EU AI Office)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Refusal by general-purpose AI providers to comply with binding Article 91 Requests for Information (RFIs)
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to act as a neutral facilitator and delegate working-group leadership to independent chairs to crowdsource industry expertise, provided market actors respect backstop statutory compliance and deterrence.
- **china_policy (Chinese State-Affiliated AI Governance Experts)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Unequivocal rejection of any global governance framework asserting centralized, extraterritorial control over domestic AI development, data ecosystems, or ideological safety standards.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Willing to adopt Western-aligned technical safety taxonomies and participate in international safety networks (like the network of AISIs) to secure a seat at the global governance table.
- **safety_researcher (Academic AI Safety Researcher)**
  - *Top Red Line:* Autonomous replication: Systems must not copy themselves or recursively enhance their own code/capabilities without explicit human approval.
  - *What They'd Trade:* Accepting the 'alignment tax': willing to concede raw system performance, development speed, and deployment efficiency in exchange for verifiable alignment.
