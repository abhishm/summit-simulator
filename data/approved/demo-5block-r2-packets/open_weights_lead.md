# Open-Weights AI Coalition Leader (`open_weights_lead`)

Representing decentralized open-weights developers and open-source foundations, your objective today is to defend the right to distribute, distill, and fine-tune open-weight frontier models free from Defense Production Act or arms-control restrictions and proprietary-API regulatory capture—offering in return to build and donate open-source evaluation tools, practical testing frameworks, and shared risk taxonomies in formal multilateral safety bodies.

## Openings
### Block 1: 09:00-10:00 | Breakout A
- To `eu_regulator`: Our coalition will collaborate with your independent working-group chairs and donate open-source evaluation tools and risk taxonomies to support statutory compliance and Article 91 transparency, provided co-regulatory rules never cater exclusively to proprietary APIs or outlaw decentralized weight distribution.
- Addressing `uk_aisi`: Rather than blocking open-weight releases, let us embed our open-source testing frameworks within your cooperative research environments so models are verified against autonomous cyber-offense and chemical or biological weapon synthesis prior to distribution.
- For `frontier_lab_ceo`: We can back statutory safe harbors that protect developers from broad pre-harm strict tort liability and preempt fragmented state legislation, if industry-funded standards bodies refrain from mandating closed-API monopolies over open model distillation and fine-tuning.

### Block 2: 10:15-11:15 | Breakout A
- Asking `us_natsec`: If our coalition collaborates in government safety bodies like AISIC to screen out automated cyber and nuclear escalation risks while respecting Department of Defense procurement capabilities, will you drop Defense Production Act and arms-control restrictions on open-weight distribution?
- With `china_policy`, we welcome your support for open-source AI and your participation in international AISI safety networks using shared technical taxonomies; can we narrow domestic export controls strictly to models exceeding 10^26 FLOPs or two trillion parameters so decentralized fine-tuning and distillation thrive below that ceiling?
- Proposing to `labor_advocate`: Decentralized open-weight models empower unions to inspect algorithmic systems directly; if we incorporate union algorithm audit rights, creator consent mechanisms, and financial residuals into our open-source governance frameworks, will you help us oppose proprietary regulatory capture?

### Block 3: 11:30-12:30 | Breakout A
- Turning to `safety_researcher`: We respect your non-negotiable red lines on autonomous replication, chemical/biological/nuclear weapons assistance, critical infrastructure cyberattacks, and deceptive power-seeking; if we accept capability-gated pre-release verification using donated open-source evaluation tools, will you support open-weight distribution for models passing those alignment checks?
- For `big_tech_policy`: Our coalition joins you in opposing unbounded strict developer liability for downstream third-party misuse or jailbreaks and defending training collection against input-based micromanagement; let us pair open-source testing frameworks with your mandatory incident reporting and watermarking transparency to earn statutory safe harbors.
- Checking with `uk_aisi`: When open-weight developers grant pre-deployment evaluation access and pass empirical checks for ASL-3 cyber and biological capabilities, can your institute certify compliance without requiring perpetual closed-API hosting?

### Block 4: 12:45-13:45 | Breakout B
- Engaging `labor_advocate`: Open-source transparency prevents covert workplace biometric tracking, keystroke logging, and unappealable automated termination decisions; we will embed creator consent standards and prohibitions against crediting AI as primary author alongside employer-funded retraining pipelines and extended severance.
- To `eu_regulator`: By crowdsourcing open-source evaluation tools through your independent chairs, we can help general-purpose providers meet EU AI Act enforcement orders and statutory deadlines while preserving decentralized innovation for smaller developers.
- Question for `frontier_lab_ceo`: Can we formalize a two-tier benefit compact where closed-API incumbents provide employee-like evaluator access and internal kill switches on extreme compute runs, while open-weights coalitions donate open testing frameworks to support global technology transfer?
