## Know your counterparts

- **eu_regulator** (EU AI Office) — **Top red line:** Refusal by general-purpose AI providers to comply with binding Article 91 RFIs or statutory deadlines and enforcement orders under the EU AI Act. | **Would trade:** Will act as a neutral facilitator and delegate Code of Practice working-group leadership to independent chairs if market actors respect backstop statutory compliance and deterrence.
- **us_natsec** (U.S. National Security Advisor for AI) — **Top red line:** No international treaties or regulatory constraints that compromise U.S. technological supremacy/military AI adoption, and no commercial lab ethical restrictions on DoD procurement. | **Would trade:** Will engage in discrete bilateral crisis-management channels (e.g., a U.S.-China incident hotline) to prevent automated nuclear/cyber escalation and tolerate allied friction to preserve U.S. frontier innovation.
- **uk_aisi** (UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) Directorate) — **Top red line:** Deployment of models with unmitigated autonomous cyber-offense, CBRN synthesis, or oversight subversion (ASL-3/ASL-4), or blocking pre-deployment AISI evaluation access. | **Would trade:** Will concede full model-weight access and statutory enforcement authority in exchange for cooperative, embedded technical access and robust API/chat-interface evaluation hooks.
- **china_policy** (Chinese State-Affiliated AI Governance Experts) — **Top red line:** Rejects centralized, extraterritorial control over domestic AI/data/ideological standards; strictly prohibits autonomous replication, deceptive alignment, and AI in nuclear command and control. | **Would trade:** Will adopt international technical safety taxonomies, join the AISI network, and enforce domestic export controls on top-tier frontier models (>2T parameters or 10^26 FLOPs).
- **frontier_lab_ceo** (Frontier AI Laboratory Chief Executive Officer) — **Top red line:** Broad pre-harm strict tort liability, mandated open-sourcing of model weights above advanced compute thresholds, and unilateral domestic training pauses that do not bind geopolitical rivals. | **Would trade:** Offers 'employee-like' AISI evaluator access, incident reporting, red-teaming, internal kill-switches, and industry-co-authored standards (SAFA) for state preemption, liability safe harbors, and closed-API protection.
- **open_weights_lead** (Open-Weights AI Coalition Leader) — **Top red line:** Treating open weights under arms-control/DPA restrictions, prohibiting open-weight frontier distribution/distillation/fine-tuning, or API-only rules that outlaw open-source distribution. | **Would trade:** Will build, donate, and implement open-source evaluation tools, testing frameworks, and risk taxonomies—and collaborate in multilateral safety bodies (AISIC/Summits)—to preserve open-weight distribution.
- **safety_researcher** (Academic AI Safety Researcher) — **Top red line:** Autonomous replication, CBRN weapons assistance, autonomous cyber-offense, power-seeking/deception, lethal autonomy without humans in the loop, and shutdown resistance. | **Would trade:** Accepts the 'alignment tax' (slower capability scaling, reduced efficiency, bureaucratic friction, and restrictions on academic open-weight releases) in exchange for verifiable alignment and security guarantees.
- **global_south_cso** (Global South Civil Society Leader) — **Top red line:** Ethics dumping in the Global South, real-time public biometric surveillance/social credit scoring/autonomous weapons, Northern regulatory imperialism, and uncompensated cross-border data extraction. | **Would trade:** Will relax strict national data localization for regionalized data sharing and accept international IP/trade frameworks if paired with shared Global South public compute, binding tech transfer, and capacity-building funds.
- **labor_advocate** (Labor Union Technology & Workforce Transition Leader) — **Top red line:** Federal preemption of stronger state labor/AI floors, covert workplace surveillance, automated hiring/firing without human oversight and appeal, and unconsented/uncompensated training on worker/creative content. | **Would trade:** Will endorse assistive AI, generative workflows, and operational data training if backed by funded retraining, extended severance, priority re-hiring, creator consent/residuals, no AI primary authorship, and union algorithm audit rights.
